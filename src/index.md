I am originally from Orlando, Florida. I graduated from Florida Southern College with a B.S. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology and a minor in mathematics. I played on the [men’s basketball team that won the 2015 NCAA Division II national championship](https://fscmocs.com/news/2015/3/28/3_28_2015_1771.aspx).

I completed an M.D. and an M.S. in Biomedical Sciences at the University of Central Florida. In Brian Kim’s bioelectronics lab I worked on in vivo electrophysiology and deep learning for neural decoding.

I am currently a diagnostic radiology resident at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences on the Early Specialization in Interventional Radiology pathway, and I serve as chief resident. I matched into independent interventional radiology at the University of Florida and am applying to neurointerventional radiology. My research is on endovascular brain–computer interfaces and imaging for device planning and targeting. I care about catheter-based neurotechnology.
