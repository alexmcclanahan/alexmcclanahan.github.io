I'm originally from Orlando, Florida. I studied biochemistry and molecular biology at Florida Southern College, and in 2015 I was on the [Florida Southern men's basketball team that won the NCAA Division II national championship](https://fscmocs.com/news/2015/3/28/3_28_2015_1771.aspx). I then completed an M.D./M.S. at the University of Central Florida, working in Brian Kim's bioelectronics lab on in vivo electrophysiology and deep learning for neural decoding. I'm a diagnostic radiology resident at UAMS (ESIR, chief resident). I matched at the University of Florida for independent interventional radiology and am applying to neurointerventional radiology this cycle.

I'm passionate about endovascular neurotechnology and its promise for clinical neuroscience and psychology.
