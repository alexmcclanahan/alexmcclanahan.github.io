I grew up in Florida, studied biochemistry and molecular biology and mathematics at Florida Southern College, and played on the 2015 [Florida Southern men's basketball team that won the NCAA Division II national championship](https://fscmocs.com/news/2015/3/28/3_28_2015_1771.aspx). I then completed my combined medical and master's degrees at the University of Central Florida, working in Brian Kim's bioelectronics lab on in vivo electrophysiology and deep learning for neural decoding, where I developed an interest in endovascular neural interfaces. Currently I'm a radiology resident at UAMS in Little Rock, AR, after which I will complete interventional radiology fellowship at UF in Gainesville, FL. I am currently applying to neurointerventional radiology fellowship.

I'm passionate about endovascular neurotechnology and its promise for neurointervention, clinical neuroscience, and psychology.
