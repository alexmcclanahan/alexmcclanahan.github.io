Originally from Orlando, Florida.

**Florida Southern College.** At Florida Southern College I earned a B.S. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology with a minor in mathematics and played on the [men’s basketball team that won the 2015 NCAA Division II national championship](https://fscmocs.com/news/2015/3/28/3_28_2015_1771.aspx).

**University of Central Florida.** An M.D. and an M.S. in Biomedical Sciences followed at the University of Central Florida. In Brian Kim’s bioelectronics laboratory, the work centered on in vivo electrophysiology and deep learning for neural decoding—an early turn toward interfaces between imaging, devices, and the brain.

**Residency and beyond.** Diagnostic radiology residency is at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences on the Early Specialization in Interventional Radiology pathway, where I serve as chief resident. A match into independent interventional radiology at the University of Florida is complete, and applications this cycle are to neurointerventional radiology. Research focuses on endovascular brain–computer interfaces and imaging for device planning and targeting, with a lasting passion for how catheter-based neurotechnology can serve clinical neuroscience.
