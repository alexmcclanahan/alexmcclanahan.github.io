I'm a diagnostic radiology resident at UAMS (PGY-4), originally from Kansas City, Missouri.

I studied biomedical sciences at UCF, then stayed for medical school (UCF College of Medicine, class of 2022). As an undergrad I interned in Dr. Peter A. Smith's lab at Nemours Children's Hospital, working on 3D printing for pediatric craniofacial reconstruction. That's what pulled me toward interventional radiology: minimally invasive, image-guided work.

Intern year was surgery at UCF/HCA Healthcare Greater Orlando. That year confirmed the path: diagnostic radiology, then IR, then endovascular surgical neuroradiology.

I'm applying this cycle to independent IR with ESIR qualification. After a VIR attending job I plan an ENI (endovascular surgical neuroradiology / neurointervention) fellowship.
